STEP 2: SHRINK THE PROJECT (free, reversible, one file)

What this does:
  Replaces src/App.tsx with a tiny placeholder page. Every old page stays in the repo
  but nothing uses it, so the app that Lovable would migrate is almost empty.
  Nothing is deleted. Your data is untouched.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT click Publish until the new site is ready.
  Your live site keeps showing the last published version until you publish.
  (This matches how your other Lovable projects work.)

STEPS:
  1. Open the Review Then Go repo on GitHub.
  2. Go to src / App.tsx and click the pencil (Edit this file).
  3. Select all, delete, paste in the contents of the App.tsx from this zip.
  4. Click Commit changes (to main).
  5. Open the project in Lovable and look at the PREVIEW. You should see
     "reviewthengo / Rebuild in progress." If you do, the shrink worked.
     If you see an error, tell me what it says. To undo, revert that commit in GitHub.

THEN (only after step 5 works):
  6. In Lovable: Project settings > General > Project actions > Migrate to TanStack Start.
  7. Read the cost card. If the price is more than you want to pay, do NOT confirm.
     Tell me the number and we will use a fresh project instead.
