## The short answer

No. According to the Government of Canada (its Europe travel page was last modified on 2 September 2026), ETIAS is not yet live. The EU will announce the specific date several months before it launches, so there is nothing for a Canadian to apply for today. You can enter Schengen countries on your Canadian passport.

## What Canada says will change later

According to the Government of Canada, once ETIAS is in place Canadians will need an authorisation for short stays in Schengen countries and Cyprus, and it advises getting it before you book tickets or accommodation. Until the EU names a date, that advice does not apply.

## Fee and validity, as reported

According to Daily Hive (10 August 2026), the planned fee is €20, about $32, and an authorisation would be valid for up to three years or until the passport expires. It also reports that travellers under 18 or over 70 pay no fee. These are press figures. We did not find them stated on a page we could read from an official source, so treat them as likely, not final.

## Why the date keeps moving

According to iVisa, the official ETIAS website dropped its "last quarter of 2026" target in July 2026 and now says ETIAS is not in operation and no applications are collected. iVisa says the European Commission has not issued a formal delay statement and that the best signals point to 2027. We could not load the official page ourselves to read the wording, so check travel-europe.europa.eu/etias before you rely on any date. Our ETIAS update article tracks the date as it changes.

## What Canadians need right now

- A passport valid for at least three months after your planned departure date, according to the Government of Canada.
- Fingerprints and a facial image at the border. According to the Government of Canada, EES is fully operational across all Schengen countries, and countries may go back to manual passport stamping during technical problems. Read how EES works at the border.
- A UK ETA if you are going to the UK. That one is live today. See our guide to the UK ETA for Canadians.

## Watch out for look-alike sites

According to the Government of Canada, be cautious of fraudulent websites that claim to sell ETIAS authorisations, and apply only through the official EU ETIAS website once the system launches. Since the system is not live, any site charging you for an ETIAS today is not offering something you need.

## What would change this answer

An official EU announcement of a launch date. We update the checked date on this page only after re-reading the sources, and public corrections are listed on the corrections page.
