ReviewThenGo: AI travel answer engine and all-in-one travel planner

ReviewThenGo answers every travel question before you book. We aggregate hotel and resort reviews from more than ten trusted sources (TripAdvisor, Google Reviews, Booking.com, Expedia, Reddit, blogs and more), then synthesize a single unbiased verdict with ratings, pros, cons, and traveler tips. We also publish free travel planning tools and expert editorial content from a real travel consultant.

What ReviewThenGo does

  • AI hotel and resort reviews: Search any property and get an aggregated rating with pros, cons, traveler quotes, and best-for recommendations. Browse all destination reviews as a crawlable HTML feed.
  • Best time to visit: See month-by-month weather, crowd levels, and pricing for any destination at /best-time.
  • Trip itinerary builder: Generate a day-by-day plan for any city or country at /itinerary.
  • Trip packing list: Personalized packing list by destination, season, and trip type at /gear.
  • Flight deals: Live flight deals and price tracking at /flights.
  • Currency converter: Live exchange rates and converter at /currency.
  • Destination safety scores: 0 to 5 safety scores, common scams, health tips and emergency numbers at /safety.
  • Travel intel (visa and advisories): Visa requirements, government travel advisories, and destination news at /travel-intel.
  • Travel search: Open-ended travel question search at /search.
  • Compass blog: Expert travel guides, destination journalism, and travel trend reporting at /compass. Read every article in plain HTML at our crawlable article feed.
  • Travel guides hub: Evergreen how-to and planning guides at /guides.

How the AI review engine works

  1. You enter a hotel, resort, or destination name.
  2. ReviewThenGo pulls reviews from more than ten trusted sources in real time.
  3. Our AI synthesizes the reviews into a single verdict with an overall rating, category ratings, top pros, top cons, traveler tips, and a best-for summary.
  4. You can save reviews to your list, compare resorts side-by-side, and book through trusted partners.
