Business: reviewthengo.com, free sourced travel rules and tools for Canadians flying to the UK and Europe.
Customer: Canadian travellers booking or about to take a trip to the UK or Europe.
Offer: free guides, tools and rule-change emails. No price.
Goal (assumed, tell me if wrong): traffic to the site and email signups, with travel insurance affiliates later once the site is large enough.

Mix: 12 of 20 hooks at levels 1 to 3 (60%), 5 at level 4 (25%), 3 at level 5 (15%).
Every fact below was checked on 14 Oct 2026 and is on the live site with its source. Re-check anything before you post it a few weeks from now. The "Fact" column is what the generator should show on screen as the source.

Funnel path for every row: social post, then profile link, then the hub page `/before-you-fly`, then the email signup, then the matching tool or guide.

## How people move down the funnel

1. Levels 1 to 3 post to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube with soft CTAs only: follow, save, share. The link in the profile goes to /before-you-fly.
2. The hub page turns a reader into an email signup. That is the only hard conversion before level 5.
3. Pin one level 4 post (4.1 or 4.3) at the top of each profile, so someone who clicks through from a level 1 to 3 post sees proof first.
4. Level 5 posts and replies go to people already asking about a delayed flight, a denied claim or a trip date.
5. Emails: first one confirms what is verified and links the three guides. Later ones point to the tool that matches the signup.
6. Travel insurance affiliate links belong at levels 3 and 4, before a trip, labelled as affiliate links, and only once the site is large enough to carry them. No endorsements or results are written until real ones exist. Use [real result] as the placeholder.
