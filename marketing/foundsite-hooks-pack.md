# Foundsite hooks pack, by awareness level

Business: foundsite.co, small Brantford, Ontario team that builds websites for local businesses.
Customer: local business owners (restaurants, trades, salons, clinics, retail, fitness).
Offer: free preview of your site before you pay. C$299 setup plus C$20 a month hosting and maintenance. Live within 48 hours. Existing site rebuilt at the same price.
Goal (assumed, tell me if wrong): owners fill out the free preview form or book the 15 minute demo.

Mix: 12 of 20 at levels 1 to 3 (60%), 5 at level 4, 3 at level 5.
Rules: no statistics, no testimonials, no results. Use [real result] or [your real story] until you have one. Every claim below is on foundsite.co today. Never quote add-on prices, they are quoted per client.

Where to post: TikTok and Reels (owners scroll them), local Facebook groups where self promotion is allowed (read each group's rules first), Google Business Profile posts. Profile link goes to foundsite.co, then the free preview form (the "I found you online" path).

## Funnel
1. Levels 1 to 3: soft calls to action only (follow, save, share). Profile link goes to foundsite.co.
2. The site now leads with the free preview, so a visitor reaches the form in one click.
3. Pin one level 4 post (4.1 or 4.4) on each profile so a visitor from a level 1 to 3 post sees proof first.
4. Level 5 posts and replies go to owners already asking about a website.
5. Outreach emails: use level 1 and 2 hooks as the opening line, and the level 4 line "you see it before you pay anything" as the offer.
6. Add a real testimonial from Walnut Landscaping or a realtor client as soon as you have one, then it becomes a level 4 post.
